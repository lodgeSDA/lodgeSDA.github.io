Our Mission

Our mission is to be a Christ-centred, welcoming community where people of every race, background, and generation can encounter God’s love, grow in faith, and serve our neighbourhood with compassion. We exist to share hope, build genuine relationships, and create a place where everyone belongs. Through worship, teaching, and practical care, we seek to make a lasting difference in our community and beyond.

Our Vision

To be a vibrant, Christ-centred community that reflects God’s love to every person in Bristol and beyond — a church where lives are transformed by the gospel, families are strengthened, and every member is equipped to serve and share the hope found in Jesus Christ.

Our History

For over seventy years, Lodge Causeway Seventh-day Adventist Church has stood at the heart of our community as a welcoming home for people of every race, background, and story. We’re a vibrant, friendly congregation, meeting on the Sabbath (Saturday) where everyone is truly valued, and newcomers are embraced like family. Our services blend heartfelt worship, practical teaching, and genuine connection, creating a space where faith can grow and lives can flourish. Whether you’re exploring church for the first time or looking for a fresh start, you’ll find warmth, openness, and a place to belong.
